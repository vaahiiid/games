Shy Sheep Hill

A shy little sheep is grazing on this hill. Hold out some grass and win her over. Every sheep is different, and every one you befriend changes the hill.

Offer
Your mouse or finger holds a tuft of grass. Click a sheep to say hello.
Slowly
Jumpy hands scare her off and she sulks for a bit. Slow, steady hands bring her closer.
Trust
Calm bites fill her five hearts. Then she lets you pet her to sleep.
Grow
Sleepy sheep join your flock, and the hill rewards you.
