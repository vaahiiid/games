Small games for big feelings

Quiet Corners

Three little games for when you're stressed, angry, or just need ten quiet minutes. There are no scores to chase, no timers and no way to lose. The only goal is to feel a little calmer than when you arrived.

When your mind is racing

Firefly Lake

Guide a firefly across a night lake. Every light you catch lets go of a kind word and sends a lantern into the sky. Hold still to breathe in, release to breathe out a wave of light, and set your worries adrift on paper boats.

  • Gentle focus
  • Kind words
  • Breathing
Play Firefly Lake

When you feel tense or angry

Shy Sheep Hill

A shy sheep only trusts slow, steady hands. Rush, and she bolts off and sulks for a bit. Move gently, and she eats from your hand, fills her five hearts and lets you pet her to sleep. Slowing your hands slows the rest of you down too.

  • Patience
  • Slow movement
  • A growing flock
Play Shy Sheep Hill

When you're restless

Moss & Sand

A small Japanese dry garden that's all yours. Rake slow lines in the sand, ripple it around stones, grow moss and watch four seasons pass. Gentle prompts give you something to do, and you can keep the gardens you love.

  • Making something
  • Slow repetition
  • Four seasons
Play Moss & Sand

Why slow games help

Slower hands

Moving slowly and smoothly is hard to do while you're wound up. Each game rewards slowness, so your body gets the message before your mind does.

Longer breaths out

A long exhale is one of the quickest ways to settle your heart rate. The games keep inviting you to breathe in, then let go.

Kinder words

Anger and stress are loud. Small, kind sentences along the way give your thoughts something gentler to hold on to.
